Which notch app fits your Mac?

Compare the job you want to do, supported macOS versions and current pricing before installing.

NotchDock

NotchDock offers sports scores, a focus timer and quick actions near the notch. The current public beta is free and requires macOS Sonoma 14 or newer. Start with a supported match or one timer.

Other options

Boring Notch is an open-source project with music, calendar and file-shelf workflows. NotchNook is another notch utility. Check each project's current documentation and offer for the features and price that matter to you.

Before you choose

  • Can you complete your main task comfortably?
  • Does the current release support your Mac and macOS version?
  • Are permissions and external data connections explained?
  • Does the current beta, trial or pricing model fit your needs?

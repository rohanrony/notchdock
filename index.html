Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about NotchDock on macOS.

What is NotchDock and how does it work?

NotchDock is a lightweight macOS desktop application that turns your MacBook hardware camera notch into an interactive status tray. By hovering your mouse cursor over the notch area at the top of your screen, NotchDock expands to show live sports scores, market watchlists, clipboard history, media controls, tasks, and silent notifications.

Who is NotchDock built for?

NotchDock is built for macOS power users, sports fans, day traders, developers, and productivity enthusiasts who want real-time status updates without cluttering their menu bar, switching desktop spaces, or dealing with intrusive popup notifications.

Why is NotchDock different from other Mac menu bar apps?

Traditional utilities clog your top menu bar or interrupt your workflow with banner alerts. NotchDock utilizes the unused screen real estate around your MacBook camera bezel, operates completely on-device with zero data tracking, and remains 100% silent until you hover your mouse.

Which Mac models and macOS versions are supported?

NotchDock supports all MacBooks running macOS Monterey (12.0) or newer, including macOS Ventura, macOS Sonoma, and macOS Sequoia. It runs natively on both Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4) MacBook Pro & MacBook Air, as well as Intel Macs.

Is NotchDock private, secure, and free?

Yes! NotchDock is completely free during public beta. It operates 100% on-device with zero cloud server dependencies, zero tracking, and zero data collection. Your clipboard text and stock watchlists never leave your Mac.

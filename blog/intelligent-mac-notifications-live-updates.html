NotchDock widgets and macOS notifications are separate

Use the macOS notification controls for app alerts. Use a NotchDock widget for its own supported sports, market, timer, and media views.

NotchDock widgets and macOS notifications are separate

Choose the right control

  • To change an app’s banners or notification access, open System Settings → Notifications and select that app.
  • To silence selected alerts temporarily, configure an allowed-app or allowed-person list in Focus.
  • To view an available NotchDock feature, open its widget; a widget is not a system notification rule.

Sports and Stocks data comes from external feeds and may be unavailable without network/provider access. NotchDock does not automatically suppress or replace macOS notifications. Apple documents per-app options in its Notifications settings guide.

NotchDock is currently free during beta and requires macOS 14 or newer.

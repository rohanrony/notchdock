Use Kaset playback controls with NotchDock

NotchDock integrates with Kaset as a media player; it is not a YouTube Music client. Kaset seeking is not available in the NotchDock widget.

Check Kaset controls in the Music widget

  1. Open Kaset and start a track.
  2. Open NotchDock’s Music widget and confirm the current item is shown.
  3. Use available playback and track controls. Like is available for Kaset; do not expect a seek control.
  4. If artwork does not load, check the player and network. Some Kaset artwork is supplied by a player URL.

NotchDock does not guarantee every control or artwork item for every track. See the supported player list and controls, and Kaset’s project documentation.

NotchDock is currently free during beta and requires macOS 14 or newer.

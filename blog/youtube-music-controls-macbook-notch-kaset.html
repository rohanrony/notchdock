How to Control YouTube Music Directly from Your MacBook Notch: Real-Time Playback, Artwork & Likes

YouTube Music listeners on macOS have long been stranded without an official native desktop app or Dynamic Island controls. Here is how pairing NotchDock with the open-source client Kaset delivers instant hover playback scrubbing, animated visualizers, dynamic album art theming, and 1-click Like buttons directly inside the MacBook camera notch bezel.

Direct Answer: Controlling YouTube Music from the MacBook Notch

To control YouTube Music from your MacBook notch, install Kaset (the native Swift/SwiftUI YouTube Music macOS client by Sertaç Özercan) and NotchDock. NotchDock connects to Kaset via local AppleScript IPC (com.sertacozercan.Kaset) to display real-time song titles, album art with dominant color glows, an interactive seek slider with persistent pause timestamps, an animated 4-bar visualizer, and a dedicated Like (👍) button right on your notch—without running heavy browser tabs or sending data to the cloud.

1. The YouTube Music on macOS Dilemma

Apple Music and Spotify users on macOS have enjoyed native desktop applications, system-wide media keys, and NowPlaying widgets for years. But for the millions of users whose primary music library lives on YouTube Music, the Mac experience has always felt neglected:

  • No Official Native App: Google has never shipped an official native desktop macOS client for YouTube Music. Users are forced to pin browser tabs in Google Chrome, Brave, or Safari.
  • Memory & Battery Drain: Keeping complex WebKit or Chromium tabs constantly open consumes hundreds of megabytes of RAM and drains battery life during mobile work sessions.
  • Lost in Tab Clutter: Pausing or skipping a track requires searching through 30 open browser tabs or jumping across macOS spaces.
  • Zero Ambient Notch Integration: Browser audio cannot dynamically adapt the MacBook camera notch with live seek bars or album art color themes.

MacBook owners with M1, M2, M3, and M4 Apple Silicon displays have a physical camera notch that remains dormant dead space during media playback. Bridging YouTube Music into that notch creates a distraction-free, glanceable music hub.

What is Kaset?

Kaset is an open-source, native macOS client for YouTube and YouTube Music built by Sertaç Özercan (sozercan/kaset on GitHub). Written purely in Swift and SwiftUI, it provides DRM playback for YouTube Music Premium, synced lyrics, WebKit lightweight isolation, a 6-band parametric equalizer, and crucial AppleScript IPC automation support.

2. How NotchDock Elevates Kaset into the Screen Notch

In the latest updates to NotchDock's Media Controller widget, we implemented first-class native integration with Kaset. Rather than relying on generic audio heuristics, NotchDock establishes a high-speed, local-first IPC bridge:

A. Real-Time Playback Scrubber with Persistent Pause State

Standard macOS media bars suffer from an annoying quirk: the moment you pause playback, the scrubber slider often resets to zero or disappears. NotchDock's custom slider continuously tracks the exact duration and elapsed seconds. When you pause a track on Kaset, the progress bar remains locked in position, allowing you to drag and seek with precision.

B. Dynamic Album Artwork Theming via Core Image

Every album has its own visual personality. When Kaset streams a new track, NotchDock extracts the high-resolution album artwork URL and passes it through an on-device CIContext and CIAreaAverage Core Image filter. The dominant hue is extracted in milliseconds and applied as an animated glowing shadow and slider accent across the notch interface.

C. Dedicated 1-Click Like (👍) Button

One of the greatest features of YouTube Music is the algorithmically curated "Liked Music" auto-playlist. When Kaset is active, NotchDock renders a dedicated thumbs-up button (👍) in the expanded notch view. Clicking it immediately executes the favoriting action inside Kaset, updating your YouTube Music library without ever opening the player window.

D. Compact 4-Bar Audio Visualizer

When collapsed, NotchDock seamlessly straddles the physical camera bezel with an animated 4-bar visualizer that bounces smoothly during playback and settles into a silent standby state when audio is paused. If paused for more than 2 minutes, the widget automatically yields its slot to keep your screen completely clean.

4. Quick 2-Minute Setup Guide

Getting your YouTube Music notch player running takes less than two minutes:

1

Install Kaset

Install Kaset via Homebrew in Terminal: brew install sozercan/repo/kaset Or download the latest release DMG from the sozercan/kaset GitHub repository.

2

Download & Launch NotchDock

Grab the free installer from notchdock.app, drag it to Applications, and launch it.

3

Grant macOS Automation Permission

When prompted by macOS, allow NotchDock to control Kaset under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation. NotchDock uses dynamic permission handling, so this only needs to be granted once.

4

Enjoy Ambient Notch Music Controls

Start playing music in Kaset. Glance at your notch to see the live visualizer, or hover your cursor to reveal full scrubber bars, playback toggles, and the Like button!

5. Architectural Engineering & Zero-Cloud Privacy

Like all NotchDock widgets, our Kaset integration is engineered around strict local-first principles:

  • No Cloud Intermediaries: NotchDock never queries external web APIs or relays song titles to an analytics backend. Communication occurs entirely over local macOS AppleScript pipes (/usr/bin/osascript).
  • Background Thread Isolation: All IPC calls are scheduled asynchronously on background dispatch queues, guaranteeing that any slow player response will never freeze your macOS cursor or drop UI frame rates.
  • Multi-Player Fallback: If you switch from Kaset to Spotify or Apple Music, NotchDock automatically detects the active player and adapts controls seamlessly.

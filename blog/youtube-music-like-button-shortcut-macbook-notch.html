Instant Track Liking: 1-Click Thumbs Up for YouTube Music in Mac Notch

Discovering an incredible new song during a coding session usually requires switching windows to hit 'Like.' Here is how to favorite tracks instantly from your camera bezel.

Direct Answer: How do you Like a YouTube Music track from the MacBook notch?

When running NotchDock paired with the Kaset desktop client, hovering over the MacBook camera notch reveals a dedicated Thumbs-Up (Like) button. Clicking it instantly communicates with YouTube Music's API to favorite the track and save it to your Liked Music playlist without switching windows.

1. Building Great Algorithms Without Friction

Recommendation algorithms rely on consistent thumbs-up feedback. By putting the Like button directly in your notch, you train your algorithm effortless without breaking focus.

Step-by-Step Setup Guide

  1. Step 1: Open Kaset and sign into YouTube Music.
  2. Step 2: Hover over the notch while any song is playing.
  3. Step 3: Click the thumbs-up icon to save to your library.

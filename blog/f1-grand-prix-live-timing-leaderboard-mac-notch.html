Can NotchDock Track Formula 1? Supported Sports on Mac

Formula 1 is not among the leagues in NotchDock’s current sports list. This page shows what is supported today and points F1 fans to a dedicated timing source.

Is Formula 1 available in NotchDock?

No. Formula 1 is not in the current supported league list. The app’s sports widget lists NBA, NFL, MLB, NHL, EPL, MLS, LaLiga, Bundesliga, FIFA World Cup 2026, and UEFA Champions League. The project roadmap lists Formula 1 as future work; that is not a release feature or a delivery commitment.

For a supported matchup, open the NotchDock sports tracker page for product details. Available games and data depend on the feed and current schedule.

Where to follow F1 timing

Use Formula 1’s own Live Timing for official session timing. NotchDock does not display that timing tower, tire data, pit information, or race-control alerts.

How this page was checked

The supported leagues listed above were checked against the current sports widget source on 2026-10-18. Formula 1 is not in that list. Recheck the current release before relying on any future feature changes.

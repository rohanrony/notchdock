Terminal Hotkey Workflows: Pairing Ghostty and iTerm2 with Notch Quick Notes

Terminal-first developers rarely touch their mouse, but need a place to stash command outputs and docker flags. Here is how to integrate NotchDock into your CLI flow.

Direct Answer: How can command-line power users integrate NotchDock with their terminal?

Developers using Ghostty, iTerm2, or Alacritty can trigger NotchDock using a custom global hotkey (e.g. Option + Space). This drops the notch tray down without requiring a trackpad, letting you paste terminal logs into Quick Notes or copy clipboard history items directly into your active shell session.

1. Keyboard-First Efficiency

While hover gestures are great for trackpad users, CLI purists can operate NotchDock entirely via keyboard bindings for rapid clipboard navigation.

Step-by-Step Setup Guide

  1. Step 1: Set your preferred global hotkey in NotchDock Settings.
  2. Step 2: Open your terminal emulator.
  3. Step 3: Summon and dismiss the notch tray instantly with keystrokes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pipe terminal stdout into NotchDock?

Yes! You can use the standard macOS pbcopy command to push CLI output directly into NotchDock's clipboard history.

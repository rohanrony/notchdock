How to Track Manchester City vs Manchester United Live on Your Mac Notch: Score Summary, Real-Time Stats & Silent Alerts

The Manchester Derby is one of the most intense fixtures in world football. If you are stuck at work, in meetings, or coding during kickoff, here is how to track Manchester City vs Manchester United live from your MacBook notch bezel without tab clutter or audio interruptions.

Direct Answer: Tracking Man City vs Man United Live on Mac

To follow Manchester City vs Manchester United live without leaving your active workspace, install NotchDock on your Mac. NotchDock transforms the physical MacBook notch into a silent live activity ticker that displays real-time scorelines (e.g., MCI 2 - 1 MUN 74'), game clocks, and subtle goal indicators. When you hover your cursor over the notch, an expanded interactive match card appears showing possession bars, shots on target, goalscorers, and lineup formations without requiring browser refreshes or streaming tabs.

The Workday Dilemma: Following the Manchester Derby While Working

When Manchester City meets Manchester United in the Premier League or cup competitions, every football fan wants to follow every minute. But weekday or weekend afternoon kickoffs present real challenges for professionals and students:

  • Video Streaming Battery Drain & Distraction: Streaming the match on a second screen or browser tab consumes gigabytes of RAM, drains battery life, and completely breaks focus during coding sprints or writing sessions.
  • The Embarrassment of Screen Sharing: If you are presenting your screen during a Zoom or Google Meet call, sudden video pop-ups or ESPN browser banners can disrupt your meeting.
  • Mobile Notification Spoilers: Phone apps like FlashScore or FotMob buzz aggressively, breaking deep work flow states and frequently spoiling goals before you even know an attack is underway.
  • Constant Manual Refreshing: Switching between Slack, IDEs, and browser tabs to check the score kills productivity and fragments your attention.

Ambient Computing to the Rescue

Instead of fighting notification fatigue or keeping cluttered tabs open, NotchDock treats the MacBook notch as zero-footprint ambient display real estate. The black camera bezel that normally sits empty becomes a live sports dashboard.

What You See in the Notch During Man City vs Man United

NotchDock is designed with a two-tier information hierarchy: a compact glanceable ticker when you are working, and an expanded match summary card when you hover.

1. The Ambient Notch Ticker (Normal State)

While you type, code, or design, NotchDock maintains an ultra-minimal footprint directly inside the camera cutout area:

  • Live Scoreline: Clean system typography showing MCI 1 - 0 MUN.
  • Match Clock: Exact minute counter including stoppage time indicators (45+3' or 88').
  • Silent Goal Glow: When Erling Haaland, Bruno Fernandes, or Kevin De Bruyne scores, a gentle emerald pulse illuminates the notch bezel without any jarring chime.

2. The Expanded Match Card (Hover State)

Move your mouse cursor to the top center of your display to instantly slide down the full interactive match center:

  • Possession Bar: Visual split showing real-time ball control percentages (e.g., Man City 62% - 38% Man United).
  • Attacking Metrics: Shots on target, total attempts, corner kicks, and expected goals (xG).
  • Goal Timeline: Player names, minute stamps, and assist details.
  • Disciplinary Record: Yellow and red card tallies to monitor match tension.

How to Set Up NotchDock for the Manchester Derby (3 Steps)

Getting ready for matchday takes less than 60 seconds on any Mac running macOS Monterey, Ventura, Sonoma, or Sequoia:

  1. Download and Open NotchDock: Grab the universal DMG from the official release page and move it into your /Applications directory.
  2. Enable the Sports Tracker Module: Open NotchDock settings by clicking the gear icon on the tray or hovering over the notch. Ensure the Sports Scores module is toggled on.
  3. Pin Manchester City or Manchester United: Under League Selection, choose English Premier League (EPL). Mark Manchester City and Manchester United as pinned clubs so the live derby score takes priority on matchday.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does NotchDock work on MacBooks without a physical notch?

Yes. On non-notch MacBooks (like M1/M2 13-inch MacBook Pro or MacBook Air) and external monitors (Studio Display, 4K/ultrawide displays), NotchDock renders an elegant, centered floating Dynamic Island bar at the top edge of your screen.

What other leagues are supported besides the Premier League?

NotchDock supports UEFA Champions League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, MLS, FIFA World Cup qualifiers, as well as NFL, NBA, MLB, and NHL games.

Will NotchDock slow down my Mac or drain the battery?

Not at all. Built entirely with Swift 6 and native SwiftUI, NotchDock consumes under 1% CPU and less than 35MB of memory. Score queries use intelligent background cooldown buffers (2s to 5s) so your network and battery stay optimal.

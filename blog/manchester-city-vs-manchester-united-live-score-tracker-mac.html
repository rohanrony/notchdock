Manchester derby scores on Mac: follow an available Premier League match

NotchDock can display a Premier League fixture when the sports feed returns it. The app does not guarantee that a Manchester City–Manchester United match is currently listed.

Check for a Premier League fixture

  1. Enable EPL in Sports Settings under Enabled Leagues.
  2. Open Sports, choose EPL, and select Today or Upcoming to browse returned fixtures.
  3. Star a team for Favourites, or pin an available non-final match from its game menu.

A derby may be unavailable because it is not on the selected date or the feed has not returned it. NotchDock shows the score details supplied by the feed; this page does not promise possession bars, shots on target, lineups, or second-by-second updates.

See the supported leagues and Sports widget controls.

NotchDock is currently free during beta and requires macOS 14 or newer.

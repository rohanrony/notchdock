View Treasury tickers in a Mac stock watchlist

  1. Open the Stocks widget.
  2. Enter a ticker in the Quick Add row; data appears only if the provider returns it.
  3. Choose a chart timeframe from the options shown in the widget.

The Stocks widget supports watchlists and selectable chart timeframes. You can check whether the provider returns data for a ticker, but quote timing and coverage may vary.

For official Treasury information, consult the relevant government source. NotchDock does not provide bond analysis or investment advice.

For current product details, see the NotchDock stock feature guide.

NotchDock is currently free during beta and requires macOS 14 or newer.

For official Treasury par yields, check the U.S. Treasury Daily Rates. The NotchDock watchlist is not an official yield source or bond analysis tool.

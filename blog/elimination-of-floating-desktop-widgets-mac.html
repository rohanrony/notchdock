Why Floating Desktop Widgets Cause Visual Friction (And Notch Anchoring Wins)

Floating timer and notes widgets seem convenient until they cover code editors, obstruct browser tabs, or require endless repositioning. Here is why hardware notch anchoring is the superior design paradigm.

Direct Answer: Why is anchoring widgets inside the MacBook notch better than floating desktop windows?

Floating desktop widgets violate basic visual hierarchy by competing for screen real estate and obscuring primary workspace applications. By contrast, NotchDock anchors utilities inside the physical, non-usable camera bezel. 1. The Fitts's Law Advantage of Screen Edges

Fitts's Law states that targets at the screen edges have infinite depth because you cannot overshoot them with your mouse cursor. The top center notch is one of the easiest targets to hit effortlessly on any Mac display.

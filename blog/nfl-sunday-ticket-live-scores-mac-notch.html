NFL Sunday on Mac: follow an available game in NotchDock

The NotchDock Sports widget supports NFL fixtures when they are present in its feed. It is a score view, not a Sunday Ticket stream or RedZone service.

Find an NFL game

  1. Enable NFL in Sports Settings under Enabled Leagues.
  2. Open Sports, choose NFL, and select Today or Upcoming to browse returned fixtures.
  3. Star a team for Favourites, or pin an available non-final match from its game menu.

If no game appears, confirm the date tab and enabled league; the feed may not have a fixture to show. NotchDock does not provide video, RedZone coverage, drive summaries, down-and-distance, or a guaranteed update interval. Use the official broadcaster or league service for those details.

See the supported leagues and Sports widget controls.

NotchDock is currently free during beta and requires macOS 14 or newer.

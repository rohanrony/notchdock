MacBook Pro Camera Notch Exact Pixel Dimensions: 14-inch vs 16-inch Breakdown

Designing software that seamlessly integrates into the MacBook camera bezel requires exact mathematical geometry. Here is the definitive engineering reference for 14-inch and 16-inch notch dimensions.

Direct Answer: What are the exact pixel dimensions of the MacBook Pro camera notch?

On the 14-inch MacBook Pro (3024×1964 native resolution), the notch measures exactly 204 logical points wide by 34 logical points high (or 408×68 physical pixels at 2x Retina scale). On the 16-inch MacBook Pro (3456×2234 native), it measures 214 points wide by 34 points high. NotchDock uses these exact coordinates via NSScreen.safeAreaInsets to align its bezel HUD pixel-perfectly.

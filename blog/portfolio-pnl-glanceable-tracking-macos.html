Direct Answer: How can I track my personal stock portfolio PnL on Mac with total privacy?

NotchDock allows you to enter your stock and crypto holdings locally on your Mac. It computes your live daily dollar PnL and percentage return directly in the camera notch. All holdings data is stored strictly in macOS UserDefaults—zero cloud transmission, zero brokerage credentials required.

1. 100% Local & Privacy-Enforced

Unlike third-party portfolio aggregators that demand Plaid logins or bank credentials, NotchDock operates completely offline for your personal holdings. You simply enter your share counts, and NotchDock fetches public market prices to calculate your net worth changes locally.

Step-by-Step Setup Guide

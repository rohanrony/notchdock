Choose which Mac notifications appear while sharing

When notifications appear during a mirrored or shared display, adjust the macOS Notification Center options for your installed version.

Choose which Mac notifications appear while sharing

Review notification behavior while sharing

  1. Open System Settings → Notifications.
  2. Review the Notification Center option for notifications when mirroring or sharing the display.
  3. Choose whether notifications are allowed during that activity; check the setting again before a presentation.
  4. For a single app, select it under Application Notifications and adjust its notification access or style.

System labels can vary by macOS version. This page describes macOS settings and makes no claim that NotchDock reads or changes iPhone Mirroring notifications. See Apple’s Notifications settings guide.

NotchDock is currently free during beta and requires macOS 14 or newer.

Market Circuit Breakers & Trading Halts: Live Alerts in Mac Notch

During extreme panic or euphoric squeezes, regulatory circuit breakers halt trading on individual stocks or the entire market. Here is how to track trading halts live on Mac.

Direct Answer: How can I monitor trading halts and stock market circuit breakers on macOS?

NotchDock flags regulatory volatility halts (LULD: Limit Up / Limit Down) and market-wide S&P 500 circuit breakers (7%, 13%, 20% drops) inside the MacBook notch. When a pinned stock is halted, the ticker turns amber with a pause badge, updating automatically when trading resumes.

1. The Mechanics of Market Halts

When a stock moves outside permitted price bands within 5 minutes, exchanges mandate a 5-minute pause. NotchDock detects halted quotes instantly and presents the reason code in the hover panel.

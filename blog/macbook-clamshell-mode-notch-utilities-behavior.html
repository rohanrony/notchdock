MacBook Clamshell Mode: How NotchDock Adapts When Your Laptop is Closed

Many professionals use their MacBook in closed-display clamshell mode connected to external monitors. Here is how NotchDock transitions seamlessly when your lid closes.

Direct Answer: What happens to NotchDock when your MacBook is in clamshell mode?

When you close your MacBook lid into clamshell mode, macOS shuts down the internal display. NotchDock instantly receives the display topology change event and migrates all active sports scores, stock watchlists, and focus timers to your primary external display as a floating Dynamic Island pill, resuming without a hitch.

1. Zero-Disruption Transition

Closing your laptop during an active sports match or Pomodoro sprint should never cancel your session. NotchDock preserves state seamlessly across all hardware transitions.

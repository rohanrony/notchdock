NBA Finals & Playoffs Live: Track Scores in Your MacBook Notch

NBA basketball is a game of rapid runs and clutch buzzer-beaters. Here is how to keep an eye on NBA Finals and playoff games live directly inside your MacBook camera cutout.

Direct Answer: How can I follow NBA playoff games live from my Mac menu or notch?

Using NotchDock, you can follow NBA playoff action live in your MacBook notch. The widget shows real-time scores (e.g. BOS 98 - 95 DAL • 4th 3:12) and possession indicators. Hovering opens an expanded box score showing leading scorers, team fouls, timeouts remaining, and shooting percentages.

1. Fast-Paced Basketball Requires Fast Updates

With 24-second shot clocks and frequent lead changes, basketball requires rapid data updates. NotchDock utilizes lightweight WebSockets and HTTP polling to ensure scoring updates arrive within seconds of the live basket.

2. Clutch Time Indicators

When games enter the final two minutes with a margin under 5 points, NotchDock highlights the scoreline in high-contrast amber, alerting you that a thrilling finish is underway.

Step-by-Step Setup Guide

  1. Step 1: Install NotchDock and move your mouse cursor over the notch.
  2. Step 2: Select the Sports module and navigate to NBA.
  3. Step 3: Pin tonight's playoff matchup.
  4. Step 4: Enjoy discreet, instant updates throughout all 4 quarters.

Wimbledon & Grand Slam Tennis Live on Mac: Sets, Games & Break Points

Grand Slam tennis matches can stretch over 4 hours with dramatic momentum swings. Here is how to track sets, games, and break points live right from your MacBook notch.

Direct Answer: How do you track Wimbledon tennis live scores and break points on a Mac?

NotchDock embeds a point-by-point tennis scoreboard in your MacBook camera bezel. It shows live set scores and active game points (e.g. ALC 6-4, 4-6, 5-4 (40-30*)). The asterisk indicates who is currently serving, while break points are highlighted in vivid green.

1. Point-by-Point Tennis Awareness

Tennis scoring is unique: 15, 30, 40, Deuce, and Advantage. NotchDock reflects every single point change in real time, giving you instant clarity on whether a server is under pressure or serving for the championship.

Step-by-Step Setup Guide

  1. Step 1: Open NotchDock and hover over the camera notch.
  2. Step 2: Select Tennis under the Sports menu.
  3. Step 3: Pick Wimbledon, Roland Garros, US Open, or Australian Open fixtures.
  4. Step 4: Pin the match for seamless set and point updates.

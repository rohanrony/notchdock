Can NotchDock track tennis scores? Current sports support on Mac

NotchDock does not currently list tennis or Grand Slam feeds in its Sports widget. The app cannot show point-by-point scoring, sets, serve indicators, or tiebreaks for tennis matches.

Can NotchDock track tennis scores? Current sports support on Mac

The current supported list is NBA, NFL, MLB, NHL, EPL, MLS, LaLiga, Bundesliga, FIFA World Cup 2026, and UEFA Champions League. Tennis is listed in the roadmap as future work; that does not mean it is available in the current app.

For a supported competition, enable its league in Sports Settings and browse its available fixtures in the expanded Sports widget.

For current product details, see the NotchDock feature guide.

NotchDock is currently free during beta and requires macOS 14 or newer.

Use a stock watchlist during CPI events

This guide does not forecast the market response to an inflation report. NotchDock’s Stocks widget is a watchlist and chart view.

Use a stock watchlist during CPI events

  1. Open the Stocks widget.
  2. Enter a ticker in the Quick Add row; data appears only if the provider returns it.
  3. Choose a chart timeframe from the options shown in the widget.

For inflation figures and release information, consult the responsible government source directly. NotchDock does not provide financial advice.

For current product details, see the NotchDock stock feature guide.

NotchDock is currently free during beta and requires macOS 14 or newer.

Check the official BLS CPI release schedule and release for current dates and figures. NotchDock does not publish the CPI report or forecast its market impact.

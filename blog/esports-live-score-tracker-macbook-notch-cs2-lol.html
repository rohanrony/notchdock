Can NotchDock track esports scores? Current sports support on Mac

NotchDock does not currently list CS2, Valorant, or League of Legends feeds. Its Sports widget currently lists NBA, NFL, MLB, NHL, EPL, MLS, LaLiga, Bundesliga, FIFA World Cup 2026, and UEFA Champions League.

Can NotchDock track esports scores? Current sports support on Mac

The current NotchDock Sports widget does not provide the esports feeds described in this article. It does not show esports maps, rounds, tournament brackets, rosters, or match alerts.

For a supported competition, enable its league in Sports Settings, select it in the expanded Sports widget, and browse available fixtures. Match availability depends on the schedule data returned by the feed.

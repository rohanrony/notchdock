Intel Mac Support: Bringing the Floating Dynamic Island Pill to Older MacBooks

Millions of users still love their Intel MacBook Pros and iMacs. You don't need a brand-new M3 or M4 laptop to enjoy ambient notch computing. Here is how Intel support works.

Direct Answer: Can you use NotchDock on Intel-based MacBooks without a physical notch?

Yes! NotchDock is distributed as a Universal Binary compiled for both ARM64 (Apple Silicon) and x86_64 (Intel). On Intel Macs without a physical camera cutout, NotchDock renders a floating Dynamic Island pill at the top of the display, giving older MacBooks the exact same interactive experience.

1. Universal Binary Architecture

We compile NotchDock for both architectures without requiring Rosetta 2 translation. Intel MacBooks execute native x86_64 code with maximum battery efficiency.

  2. Step 2: Install on your Intel MacBook, iMac, or Mac mini.

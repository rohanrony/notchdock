Crypto Fear & Greed Index: Live Ambient Gauge in Your MacBook Notch

Warren Buffett famously advised being fearful when others are greedy, and greedy when others are fearful. Here is how to track the Crypto Fear & Greed Index live in your MacBook notch.

Direct Answer: How do you track the Crypto Fear & Greed Index on a Mac?

With NotchDock, the Crypto Fear & Greed Index sits as an ambient numeric badge (e.g. FG: 74 • Greed) in your MacBook notch. Color-coded gradients transition from deep red (Extreme Fear: 0-25) to vibrant green (Extreme Greed: 75-100), giving you instant sentiment context.

1. The Psychology of Market Extremes

Market extremes often mark cyclical tops and bottoms. By keeping the sentiment gauge in your peripheral vision, you make smarter, more rational allocation decisions.

Step-by-Step Setup Guide

  1. Step 1: Open NotchDock on your Mac.
  2. Step 2: Hover over the notch and navigate to Crypto -> Sentiment.
  3. Step 3: Pin the Fear & Greed Index.
  4. Step 4: Check daily sentiment changes with a simple glance.

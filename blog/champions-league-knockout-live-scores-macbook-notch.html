Follow UEFA Champions League fixtures in NotchDock

The Sports widget can show available UEFA Champions League fixtures. It does not promise aggregate totals, penalty tracking, or a particular live match feed.

Find a Champions League fixture

  1. Enable UEFA Champions League in Sports Settings under Enabled Leagues.
  2. Open Sports, choose the competition, and select Today or Upcoming to browse returned fixtures.
  3. Use the star control on a team row to add it to Favourites, or use the game menu to pin a non-final match.

If the fixture list is empty, check the selected date tab and enabled league. The feed may have no available fixtures. The widget displays the match details it receives; it does not provide the aggregate score or penalty-by-penalty details claimed in the older version of this article.

See the supported leagues and Sports widget controls.

FOMC Fed Rate Decision Live: How to Track the 2:00 PM Market Reaction on Mac

Every six weeks, the Federal Reserve's 2:00 PM ET interest rate decision sends shockwaves through global equities, currencies, and bonds. Here is how to follow the market reaction live inside your MacBook notch.

Direct Answer: How can I track the FOMC interest rate decision and market reaction live on macOS?

With NotchDock, you can follow FOMC rate decisions and Fed Chair Jerome Powell's press conference live in your MacBook notch. At 2:00 PM ET, NotchDock captures instant price ticks across the S&P 500 (^GSPC), 10-Year Treasury Yields (^TNX), and the US Dollar Index (DXY), rendering green/red intraday sparklines directly in the camera bezel.

1. The 2:00 PM Algorithmic Whip-Saw

When the Federal Open Market Committee drops its policy statement, high-frequency trading algorithms parse the text in microseconds. Equities can swing 1% in either direction in seconds before Powell even steps to the microphone at 2:30 PM. Having a live, low-latency market ticker embedded in your hardware notch lets you maintain full situational awareness while you work.

2. Multi-Asset Reaction in Pure AppKit

Understanding the Fed's stance requires monitoring multiple correlated assets simultaneously:

  • S&P 500 Futures (ES): Equity risk sentiment.
  • US 10-Year Treasury Yield (^TNX): Bond market rate expectations.
  • US Dollar Index (DXY): Global currency liquidity.
  • Gold (XAU/USD): Inflation and safe-haven flows.

NotchDock displays these four metrics in a consolidated, hover-expandable panel.

Step-by-Step Setup Guide

  1. Step 1: Install NotchDock on your Mac.
  2. Step 2: Hover over the notch and click the Stocks graph icon.
  3. Step 3: Add ^GSPC, ^TNX, and DXY to your pinned watchlist.
  4. Step 4: Enable 'High-Frequency Refresh' during FOMC release days.

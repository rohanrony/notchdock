Direct Answer: How do you track March Madness games and upsets live in the Mac notch?

NotchDock features a multi-game NCAA tournament mode for March Madness. It rotates through active tournament games or pins your top bracket seed directly in the MacBook notch. When an underdog is within 3 points in the final 4 minutes, an Upset Alert badge lights up in high-contrast amber.

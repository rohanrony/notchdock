View supported sports scores and stock watchlists on Mac

The app does not promise every sports competition, live market alert, candlestick or bid/ask detail, or fixed resource usage.

View supported sports scores and stock watchlists on Mac

  1. Open the Stocks widget.
  2. Enter a ticker in the Quick Add row; data appears only if the provider returns it.
  3. Choose a chart timeframe from the options shown in the widget.

For sports, use one of the currently listed leagues in the Sports widget. For markets, add tickers to a Stocks watchlist and select a chart timeframe.

Sports schedules and quote data are supplied by external services and may be unavailable or delayed. Read the product guides for current controls.

For current product details, see the NotchDock sports feature guide.

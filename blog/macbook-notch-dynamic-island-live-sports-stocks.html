How to Turn Your MacBook Notch Into an Always-On Live Activity Center for Sports, Stocks & Tasks

Want iPhone-style Live Activities and Dynamic Island capabilities on your MacBook? Here is how to follow live sports goals, track intraday market swings, and manage clipboard history right underneath the camera bezel.

Direct Answer: How to Get Live Sports & Stocks in the Mac Notch

To get real-time sports scores and stock tickers directly in your MacBook notch, install NotchDock (a free lightweight macOS application). NotchDock docks into the hardware camera cutout, streaming live match updates (Premier League, NFL, NBA, MLB) and financial index sparklines (S&P 500, NASDAQ, crypto) into a subtle status ticker. Hovering over the notch opens full scoreboards, play stats, and candlestick charts without launching separate browser tabs.

Why Mac Power Users Love Live Activities in the Notch

When following an afternoon Premier League fixture or monitoring high-volatility market movements during trading hours, the traditional Mac experience is frustrating:

  • Browser Tab Clutter: Keeping 3 ESPN tabs or TradingView charts open hogs RAM and clutters your workspace.
  • Notification Interruption: Enabling phone push alerts causes your Apple Watch and iPhone to buzz every time a goal or price threshold triggers.
  • Menu Bar Limitations: Menu bar stock tickers truncate company names and cannot render full sparklines or match lineups.

By integrating an ambient Live Activity engine directly into the black notch bezel, NotchDock delivers glanceable updates in zero screen space.

Step 1: Download & Install NotchDock

NotchDock is distributed as a universal signed DMG package compatible with macOS Monterey (12.0) through macOS Sequoia (15.0+):

  1. Download NotchDock.dmg from our official releases page.
  2. Drag NotchDock.app to your /Applications folder.
  3. Launch the application and grant macOS Accessibility permissions in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility (this allows the app to detect mouse hover coordinates around the camera bezel).

Step 2: Configuring Live Sports Score Tracking

NotchDock includes automated live score polling for all major international sports leagues:

  • Football (Soccer): English Premier League (EPL), UEFA Champions League, La Liga, Serie A, MLS, and FIFA tournaments.
  • American Football: NFL live scoreboards, game clock, down-and-distance indicators.
  • Basketball & Baseball: NBA live quarter scores and MLB inning-by-inning updates.

When a live game is active, NotchDock renders a compact ticker string like ARS 1 - 0 CHE 34' directly in the notch bezel. Hovering over the notch opens the expanded scoreboard card with possession bars, goal events, and team emblems.

Step 3: Setting Up Intraday Stock & Crypto Watchlists

Day traders, tech professionals, and crypto enthusiasts can track custom equity baskets with zero API configuration:

  • Major Indices: S&P 500 (^GSPC), NASDAQ Composite (^IXIC), Dow Jones Industrial Average (^DJI).
  • Equities & Tech Stocks: AAPL, NVDA, MSFT, TSLA, GOOGL, with instant high/low intraday range bars.
  • Crypto Watchlists: Real-time BTC/USD, ETH/USD, and SOL/USD market prices.

Ambient Green/Red Sentiment Indicators

When a tracked equity is up for the day, a soft green accent glow highlights the notch ticker. When markets retreat, a subtle neutral amber indicator appears, allowing you to gauge market sentiment in a fraction of a second without opening financial portals.

Step 4: Leveraging the Quick Clipboard Manager

Beyond live sports and market tickers, NotchDock includes a built-in clipboard snippet history manager:

  • Copies stay strictly local in memory on your Mac (zero cloud sync).
  • One-click paste for terminal commands, API keys, email templates, and hex color codes.
  • Filter clipboard history by text, links, or code snippets with lightning-fast search.

Conclusion: Maximize Every Millimeter of Your Mac Display

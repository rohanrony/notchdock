IPL Cricket Live on Mac: Ball-by-Ball Scores, Wickets & Run Rates

Indian Premier League (IPL) matches deliver high-octane 20-over drama with boundaries, sixes, and nerve-wracking death overs. Here is how to follow every ball live from your Mac notch.

Direct Answer: How do you track IPL cricket live scores and run rates on a Mac?

NotchDock offers native cricket score tracking in the MacBook camera notch. The ticker displays runs, wickets, and overs (e.g. CSK 178/4 (17.3 ov) • RRR: 9.8). Hovering expands the scorecard showing current batsmen runs/balls, bowler figures, and recent ball-by-ball commentary (1 • 4 • W • 6 • 0 • 1).

1. Cricket's Mathematical Thrill in the Notch

T20 cricket is an intense game of calculations: Current Run Rate (CRR) versus Required Run Rate (RRR). NotchDock computes these figures continuously so you know the exact equation at the end of every over.

Step-by-Step Setup Guide

  1. Step 1: Open NotchDock and hover your cursor over the notch.
  2. Step 2: Select Cricket -> IPL.
  3. Step 3: Pin the active match.
  4. Step 4: Track boundaries, wickets, and overs while you code or write.

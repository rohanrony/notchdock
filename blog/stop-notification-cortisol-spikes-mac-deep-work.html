Reduce Mac notification interruptions: a practical checklist

Make a notification plan for one work block

  1. List the apps whose alerts need an immediate response and leave those notifications available.
  2. In System Settings → Notifications, adjust the alert style for one lower-priority app.
  3. If you need a temporary quiet period, configure a Focus with the people and apps you still want to hear from.
  4. After the work block, review missed alerts and adjust the allow list.

This workflow does not claim a health effect. NotchDock’s timer can mark a work interval, but it does not change notification settings or turn Focus on automatically. Read Apple’s Notifications guide and Focus guide.

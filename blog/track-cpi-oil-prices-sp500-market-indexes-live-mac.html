Use a market watchlist for index and commodity tickers

The Stocks widget can display tickers and charts when the quote provider returns the data. It does not interpret economic releases or predict market direction.

Use a market watchlist for index and commodity tickers

  1. Open the Stocks widget.
  2. Enter a ticker in the Quick Add row; data appears only if the provider returns it.
  3. Choose a chart timeframe from the options shown in the widget.

For official economic statistics and commodity information, consult the source agency or exchange. NotchDock is not a financial research or trading service.

For current product details, see the NotchDock stock feature guide.

NotchDock is currently free during beta and requires macOS 14 or newer.

For event dates and source data, consult the BLS CPI schedule and the U.S. Energy Information Administration’s weekly petroleum report. The NotchDock watchlist does not combine these releases into a market analysis.

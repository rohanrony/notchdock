MLB World Series Live on Mac: Innings, Outs & Pitch Count in Notch

Baseball is a game of patience, tension, and sudden fireworks. Here is how to follow the MLB World Series with base runners, count, and outs tracked directly in your MacBook notch.

Direct Answer: How can I follow MLB baseball games with outs and base runners on my Mac?

NotchDock features an integrated MLB diamond widget inside the MacBook notch. It displays the active score, inning (e.g. ▲ 7th), count (3-2), and outs (● ● ○). Hovering over the notch reveals an interactive base runner diamond showing occupied bases, current pitcher strikeout counts, and upcoming batters.

1. Why Baseball is Ideal for Ambient Tracking

With long breaks between pitches and 9 full innings, baseball is the ideal sport for ambient computing. You don't need a constant video feed playing; you just need to know when someone is on base, what the count is, and when a home run clears the fences.

2. The Visual Diamond in AppKit

NotchDock renders a tiny, elegant diamond showing 1st, 2nd, and 3rd base. When a runner reaches scoring position, the diamond lights up in high-contrast Apple gold, alerting you that a scoring threat is live.

Step-by-Step Setup Guide

  1. Step 1: Open NotchDock on your Mac.
  2. Step 2: Hover over the notch and navigate to MLB.
  3. Step 3: Select the World Series game or your home team.
  4. Step 4: Pin the match for live base runner and inning updates.

How Does the Notch Stock Ticker Work?

NotchDock Stock Ticker transforms your MacBook notch into an aesthetic financial dashboard. When market hours open, the notch displays live pricing, price delta (+/- percent gainers/losers), and high/low intraday range bars on simple mouse hover.

Key Capabilities for Investors & Traders

World Indices & Equities

Track NASDAQ (^IXIC), Dow Jones, S&P 500, tech giants, or custom tickers with instantaneous delta color coding.

Intraday Range Bar

Visual range bar showing current position relative to day low and day high prices directly in the hover tray.

Privacy-First Local Storage

Your custom watchlist symbols stay 100% on your Mac using local storage. Zero third-party tracking.

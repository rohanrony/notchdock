Stocks & Indices Module

Track Real-Time Stocks & Indices Under Your MacBook Notch

Keep an eye on market trends, NASDAQ, S&P 500, equity watchlists, and price fluctuations without opening heavy trading terminals or keeping browser tabs pinned.

Direct Answer: Tracking Stocks on MacBook Notch

NotchDock Stock Ticker transforms your MacBook screen notch into a real-time financial ticker dashboard. Pin S&P 500, NASDAQ indices, individual stock equities (AAPL, TSLA, NVDA), or cryptocurrencies (BTC, ETH). Hovering over the notch displays intraday high/low range bars, real-time percentage changes, and price deltas silently without interrupting your active desktop apps.

Key Capabilities for Investors & Traders

Built for long-term index investors, tech stock tracking, and day traders who want real-time financial data without keeping TradingView or Yahoo Finance open continuously.

S&P 500 & Global Index Ticker

Monitor S&P 500 (^GSPC), NASDAQ 100 (^NDX), and Dow Jones (^DJI) with live point changes and green/red status indicators.

Tech Stock Watchlist

Track Apple (AAPL), Nvidia (NVDA), Tesla (TSLA), Microsoft (MSFT), and custom tickers right from your screen bezel.

Bitcoin & Crypto Watch

Track real-time Bitcoin (BTC/USD), Ethereum (ETH/USD), and major crypto rates alongside traditional stocks.

Intraday High/Low Range Bars

Visual range bars showing exact current pricing relative to the day's low and high bounds inside the hover tray.

Discreet Office & Meeting View

Glance at market movements during office hours or video calls without revealing your portfolio to colleagues.

100% On-Device Privacy

Watchlists are stored purely in local macOS memory. No cloud sync, no tracking, no data sharing.

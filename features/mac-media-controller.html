Direct Answer: Media Controls on MacBook Notch

NotchDock Media Controller embeds universal media controls, album cover art, track progress scrubbers, and play/pause buttons directly inside the MacBook screen notch. Powered by native macOS NowPlaying APIs, it seamlessly controls Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube web audio, and podcast apps on hover without switching away from your active work app.

Intelligent Silent Notch Notifications for MacBook

Reclaim your focus. NotchDock transforms the hardware display notch into a ambient, non-intrusive status area that delivers information at a glance without loud alerts or annoying banners.

Why Silent Notch Notifications Matter

Traditional operating system notifications constantly fight for your attention. NotchDock's Silent Notch Philosophy integrates status updates into unused screen bezel space. Information remains available whenever you choose to glance or hover, never interrupting active coding, writing, or presenting.

Minimalist Notification Principles

Glanceable Status Dots

Subtle pulse dots and mini counters signal ongoing sports scores, active timers, or media sessions.

Hover-to-Reveal Tray

Hovering your mouse over the notch expands rich interactive widgets without launching separate desktop windows.

Zero Chimes or Banners

Protects your screen recorded tutorials, live presentations, and focused deep work from embarrassing popup notifications.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does NotchDock replace macOS Notification Center?

No. NotchDock runs alongside macOS Notification Center, handling live status widgets (sports, stocks, media, timers) quietly near the notch.

Works on MacBooks without camera notches?

Yes! On external monitors or standard MacBooks, NotchDock creates a sleek top-center status bar overlay that emulates the notch UX.

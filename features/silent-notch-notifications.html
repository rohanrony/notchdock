Direct Answer: Silent Notifications on MacBook Notch

NotchDock Silent Notch Notifications replace intrusive banner popups and audio chimes on macOS with subtle ambient glow indicators and dynamic status tickers built directly into the black MacBook camera cutout bezel. Status information (sports scores, stock alerts, render progress, timers) remains visible on glance or hover without interrupting active screens, video calls, or focus sessions.

No. NotchDock runs alongside macOS Notification Center, handling live status widgets (sports, stocks, media, timers) quietly near the notch.

Works on MacBooks without camera notches?

Yes! On external monitors or standard MacBooks, NotchDock creates a sleek top-center status bar overlay that emulates the notch UX.

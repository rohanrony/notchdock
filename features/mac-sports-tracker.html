Direct Answer: Live Sports Scores on macOS Notch

NotchDock Live Sports Score Tracker is a specialized macOS desktop utility that transforms the MacBook camera hardware notch (M1/M2/M3/M4 & Intel Macs) into a real-time live score ticker. Moving your mouse over the screen cutout expands a hover tray displaying match minutes, team scores, and game statistics across Premier League, Champions League, NFL, NBA, and MLB—all updated silently without notification popups.

How It Works on macOS

NotchDock seamlessly integrates with your MacBook screen hardware cutouts:

  • Pulse Score Ticker: A compact, live score ticker sits neatly beside your camera notch when collapsed.
  • Interactive Hover Tray: Moving your mouse over the notch expands the full match center with live possession, scorelines, and match clocks.
  • Automatic Feed Refresh: Ultra-fast background API sync with minimal CPU & memory footprint.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I setup live sports tracking on NotchDock?

Download NotchDock for macOS, open the settings menu from your notch, and select your preferred leagues or pinned games. Scores update automatically.

Does NotchDock drain MacBook battery?

No. NotchDock is optimized using native macOS APIs, requiring negligible CPU and battery resources during live game tracking.

Can I hide the sports tracker when games end?

Yes. The tracker dynamically hides when no active games are pinned or when you toggle off the live sports module in preferences.

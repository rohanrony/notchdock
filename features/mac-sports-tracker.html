Live Sports Module

Track Real-Time Sports Scores Directly on Your MacBook Notch

Follow live matches, game timers, team stats, and tournament scores seamlessly underneath your MacBook display cutout. NotchDock gives sports fans instant score visibility without distracting browser tabs or loud notifications.

What is the NotchDock Live Sports Tracker?

NotchDock Live Sports Tracker is a native macOS status bar widget designed specifically for MacBook Pro and MacBook Air notches. It embeds live scores into your screen's notch region, providing a subtle pulse indicator for ongoing games and expanding on hover to reveal detailed team statistics, scoreboards, and match clocks.

Key Use Cases & Capabilities

Soccer & Football Leagues

Track live match minutes (e.g. 59'), goals, and group stage standings across international tournaments, Champions League, and top domestic leagues.

NBA & College Basketball

Stay updated on quarter-by-quarter NBA scores and tight game finishes while coding, designing, or studying on your Mac.

Silent Notification Philosophy

No banner popups or sound disruptions. The notch quietly updates in real time, keeping your screen minimalist and work uninterrupted.

How It Works on macOS

NotchDock seamlessly integrates with your MacBook screen hardware cutouts:

  • Pulse Score Indicator: When collapsed, a compact live score ticker sits neatly beside your camera notch.
  • Interactive Hover Tray: Moving your cursor over the notch expands the full match center with live possession, detailed scores, and referee timers.
  • Automatic Feed Refresh: Powered by fast public sports data APIs with minimal system memory footprint.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I setup live sports tracking on NotchDock?

Download NotchDock for macOS, open the settings menu from your notch, and select your preferred leagues or pinned games. Scores update automatically.

Does NotchDock drain MacBook battery?

No. NotchDock is optimized using native macOS APIs, requiring negligible CPU and battery resources during live game tracking.

Can I hide the sports tracker when games end?

Yes. The tracker dynamically hides when no active games are pinned or when you toggle off the live sports module in preferences.
